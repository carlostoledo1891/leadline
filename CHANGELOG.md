# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-11-10, the starter

- `leadline.mpl`: cert-machine's matplotlib kit (WARRANT v2.3, the print register), copied byte for byte and pinned.
- `leadline.plotly`: the same register for plotly — templates `warrant` and `warrant-grey`, the same helpers and refusals;
  legends and colour bars under the plot, read at 390 px.
- `leadline.fields`: a proposal — three field roles (magnitude `oslo`, difference `bam`, direction `bamO`) with the
  measurements behind them (`fields-report.json`).
- `tokens/leadline.tokens.json`: design tokens for Figma, generated.
- Four examples in both libraries; 29 checks with red controls.
